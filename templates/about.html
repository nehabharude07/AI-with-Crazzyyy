About us

Engineers first. AI second. Hype never.

AI.WITH CRAZZYYY started as one simple frustration: too many "AI projects" were slide decks with no working code behind them. We build the opposite — industry-grade AI, ML and generative AI systems using advanced techniques like LangChain and LangGraph, solving real business problems for real teams.

Our story

Year 1

Started as a Python automation consultancy

Small businesses needed their spreadsheets and manual reports turned into scripts that ran themselves. We obliged, one script at a time.

Year 2

Moved into machine learning

Automation clients started asking for predictions, not just pipelines — churn, demand, pricing. We built our first models into production.

Year 3

Added generative AI & GenAI assistants

As LLMs matured, we started building retrieval-augmented assistants and copilots directly into clients' existing tools.

Today

Teaching what we build

We now mentor engineers and run cohort-based training, because the fastest way to scale AI adoption is to teach people to build it themselves.

What we believe

01

Shipped beats polished

A working model in production teaches you more than a perfect one in a notebook.

02

Explain it or it doesn't count

If we can't explain how a model reaches a decision, we don't ship it as-is.

03

Automate the boring, not the important

We automate the repetitive work so your team can spend time on the judgment calls.

04

Teach as you go

Every engagement leaves your team more capable than it found them.
